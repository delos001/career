# Operations Strategy - CV Framing Rules

**Used by:** cv_targeted, axis-classifier

## Capability vocabulary

- Operating-model design across the five components (structure, processes, people/talent, technology, governance)
- Target Operating Model (TOM) development; current-state ("as-is") and future-state ("to-be") modeling; transition-state design
- Decision-rights design and RACI/RAPID architecture for cross-functional functions
- Governance framework design (controlled documentation, policies, standard libraries, decision forums, steering bodies)
- Capability strategy and capability mapping (capability hexagon: mission, insights, processes, technologies, talent, governance)
- Capability roadmapping linking strategy to phased capability buildout
- Functional and organizational structural model design (capability pillars, role definitions, span/layers viewed through a capability lens)
- Process architecture (workflow design, end-to-end process mapping, swimlane modeling)
- Process improvement and operational excellence (Lean, Six Sigma, BPM): SIPOC, value stream mapping (VSM), DMAIC diagnostic application, Theory of Constraints
- Technology strategy: platform evaluation criteria design, multi-vendor evaluation, platform-selection recommendation, technology-roadmap design
- Sourcing and vendor strategy (preferred-partner models, FSP/FSO governance, multi-vendor rationalization)
- Transformation program design (program charters, scope definition, phased program governance, risk frameworks)
- Project charter authoring and approval; objectives definition and granular requirements
- Cross-functional capability program leadership at function or portfolio level
- Strategic narrative development for functional groups (roadmaps, leadership communications, budget alignment)
- Benchmarking against external organizations to inform structural and capability decisions
- KPI and outcome-metric architecture for operating-model effectiveness (cycle time, cost, quality, capability-realization)

## Terminology

- Operating model components (Galbraith Star, McKinsey 7-S adjacent): structure, processes, people/talent, technology, governance, performance
- Target Operating Model (TOM); current-state vs future-state; transition state
- Capability map, capability taxonomy, capability hexagon (mission, insights, processes, technologies, talent, governance)
- Decision rights, RACI, RAPID; decision forums, governance bodies, steering committees
- Process improvement: SIPOC, value stream mapping (VSM), DMAIC, DMADV, Kaizen, A3 problem-solving, Lean principles, Six Sigma, Theory of Constraints
- BPM (Business Process Management); end-to-end process; process owner; process taxonomy
- Strategic frameworks: Porter's Five Forces (operating-context application), McKinsey 7-S, capability-based strategy, business architecture
- Transformation program governance: program charter, steering committee, OKR/KPI architecture, milestone gating, stage-gate review
- Sourcing models: in-house, outsourced, FSP (functional service provider), FSO (full-service outsourcing), preferred-partner, multi-vendor rationalization
- Roadmaps: capability roadmap, technology roadmap, transformation roadmap, functional roadmap
- Benchmarking: external benchmarking, peer-group analysis, maturity model assessment
- Project artifacts: project charter, scope statement, business case, requirements document, evaluation criteria matrix

## Knowledge-transfer mode

- Training delivery, curriculum design, and adoption coaching on this specialty's capabilities, methods, tools, or artifacts, when concurrently practicing the specialty in the role.

## Adjacency

Translation signal for entries tagged with adjacent specialties:

- **people-leadership**: org-design work that defines functional structure and capability pillars (operations-strategy) often co-occurs with the people-leadership work of building, hiring, and managing the team that fills the structure. Both can co-tag when the candidate did both.
- **clinical-operations**: when the operating-model or capability work is anchored in a clinical functional area (data acquisition, monitoring, study management), clinical-operations co-tags as the domain context. Operations-strategy carries the structural-design layer; clinical-operations carries the domain anchor.
- **quality-compliance**: when operating-model work intersects with QMS/GxP framing (CAPA, validation, GxP-aligned governance), quality-compliance co-tags as the regulatory framing layer. Operations-strategy carries the broader operating-model design; quality-compliance carries the GxP-anchored governance.
- **data-engineering** / **ai-engineering**: when capability or technology strategy decisions feed directly into platform implementation the candidate also performed, the engineering specialty co-tags for the build layer.
