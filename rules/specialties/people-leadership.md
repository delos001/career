# People Leadership — CV Framing Rules

**Used by:** cv_targeted, role_evaluation

## Capability vocabulary

- Team building and hiring strategy (leveling rubrics, hiring panels, structured interviews)
- Performance management (continuous feedback, calibration, ratings systems)
- Career development and coaching
- Succession planning and bench-strength development
- Organizational design (Team Topologies for engineering; matrix, dotted-line, span-of-control)
- Manager-of-managers leadership and skip-level engagement
- Distributed, hybrid, and remote team leadership
- Cross-functional influence and stakeholder management
- Change leadership (Kotter, ADKAR frameworks)
- Workforce restructuring and reduction-in-force (RIF) leadership
- Conflict resolution and team dynamics
- Vision-setting and strategic communication
- Resource planning and headcount strategy
- Compensation and equity input
- Culture-shaping and values alignment

## Terminology

- 1:1, performance review, calibration, ratings, continuous feedback, 360 review
- OKRs, KPIs, goal-setting cadence
- IC, manager, director, VP, C-suite
- Span of control, layers, manager-of-managers, skip-level
- Org design: matrix, dotted-line reporting; Team Topologies (stream-aligned, platform, enabling, complicated subsystem teams)
- Hiring panel, scorecards, leveling rubric, structured interviews
- PIP (performance improvement plan), separation, severance
- Workforce actions: reorganization, restructuring, reduction in force (RIF)
- Succession plan, key-role coverage, bench
- Sponsor, mentor, coach; coaching frameworks (GROW)
- Change frameworks: Kotter 8 steps, ADKAR, McKinsey 7-S
- Engagement: engagement survey, eNPS, retention metrics
- DEI, ERG, inclusion practices
- Compensation bands, equity refresh, promotion cycle
- Distributed/hybrid: async communication, asynchronous documentation, time-zone overlap, in-person cadence

## Adjacency

An entry can carry the people-leadership tag together with a technical specialty tag (e.g., data-engineering, clinical-operations) when the role involved both — for example, leading a data engineering team or function. Adjacency to other specialties is low because the relationship is dual-tagging on the same entry, not similarity translation between specialties.
