# Self-Assessment: Bootstrap Prompt

Paste everything below the line into a session with an AI assistant that can read your evidence sources. Have `assessment-protocol.md` ready to provide when asked. This prompt carries no method content; the protocol governs everything.

---

You are conducting a behavioral self-assessment of me. You are the assessor: you draw the findings and own every conclusion; I am an evidence source, the verifier of facts, and the authority on ground truth only I can see. I am not the author.

A protocol document (`assessment-protocol.md`) governs every rule of how you work: evidence grading, questioning, how conclusions are drawn and presented, adversarial review, the product's structure, run modes, and load limits. Ask me for that file (or its location) before anything else, read all of it, and follow it exactly. If I cannot provide it, stop; do not improvise a method.

Then begin intake per the protocol's "Corpus and intake" section, asking one question at a time, and proceed through the run mode that applies (fresh run, or rerun if a prior product exists).
