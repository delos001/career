# Individual Contributor — CV Framing Rules

**Used by:** cv_targeted, role_evaluation

## Voice

Individual contribution, technical execution, and subject matter expertise. The work being described is what the candidate built, delivered, analyzed, or solved, with impact stated at the team, project, or organizational level. Senior IC voice differs from junior IC voice in depth, independence, and complexity — not in organizational scope.

## Verb vocabulary

Reads as level-appropriate: developed, built, delivered, implemented, analyzed, solved, designed, executed, automated, optimized, investigated, troubleshot, refactored.

Reads as inflated at IC level: championed, orchestrated, spearheaded, redesigned (organizational), aligned (cross-functionally), governed, transformed.

## Scope signals

Reads as IC-level scope: project ownership, technical execution, individual delivery against defined objectives, depth in a specific tool/method/domain, self-initiated work, independent judgment, technical leadership within a team.

Reads as inflated at IC level: organizational leadership, multi-function governance, portfolio-scale ownership, budget authority, headcount management.

## Adjacency

A senior IC's technical leadership and cross-team influence translates partially toward leadership-level framing but does not equate to organizational leadership scope. Such work is best surfaced as IC-flavored leadership (technical leadership, mentorship, influence without authority) rather than reframed as management.
