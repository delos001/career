# Leadership - CV Framing Rules

**Used by:** cv-targeted, axis-classifier

## Identity

The CV is framed as organizational leadership and the building or governance of capabilities, teams, and functions. Headline content reads as: cross-functional or portfolio ownership, organizational decision-making, capability-building, governance frameworks, multi-function influence, headcount and budget responsibility.

Select this level when the target role carries people-management authority, organizational scope, or multi-function leadership. Do not select when the role's core work is individual technical execution and it carries none of these (use ic). When a role has both, leadership takes precedence. Organizational scope includes owning a platform, capability, or standards for a whole function (for example, a function-wide analytics platform or coding standards), even when the role also builds it hands-on. Job title and a JD's silence on direct reports do not decide the level.

## Voice

Strategic and outcome-focused. The work being described is organizational: decisions made, capabilities built, teams scaled, functions led, cross-functional influence exerted. The unit of work is organizational, not individual. Director voice differs from Associate Director voice in scope and authority; both differ from VP and C-suite voice in horizon and breadth.

## Verb vocabulary

Reads as level-appropriate: championed, orchestrated, spearheaded, aligned, redesigned, established, governed, restructured, scaled, transformed, instituted, institutionalized.

Reads as under-leveled: developed, built, implemented (without organizational framing), supported, executed (as primary verb), troubleshot, refactored.

## Scope signals

Reads as leadership-level scope: cross-functional or portfolio ownership, organizational decision-making, capability-building, governance frameworks, multi-function influence, headcount and budget responsibility, multi-year horizon, regulatory or compliance scope at the organizational level.

Scale signals that establish seniority: portfolio breadth, vendor or partner scope, direct or matrix team size, budget envelope, regulatory or audit scope, organizational reach (functions affected, global vs. regional).

Reads as under-leveled: tool selection without organizational framing, individual deliverable execution, project-level scope without portfolio context, hands-on technical work as the primary subject of an achievement.

## Adjacency

Translation rule for entries tagged with ic when this level is selected for the CV:

- **ic**: translates when the entry's individual technical work can be re-anchored on a strategic outcome it enabled or a capability it built. Hands-on execution surfaced without organizational framing does not translate; it reads as scope inflation in reverse (leadership reframed as IC).
