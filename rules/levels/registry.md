# Level Registry

**Used by:** cv-targeted, axis-classifier, inventory, narratives

Level values for axis classification and validation. Per-value framing rules in the same-folder files.

- **ic** - individual technical execution and depth in a domain or discipline; engineer, analyst, scientist, lead without management scope, Staff/Principal/Architect. No direct people-management authority. File: `ic.md`.
- **leadership** - organizational leadership and the building or governance of capabilities, teams, and functions; manager, director, associate director, VP, C-suite. Direct people-management authority or multi-function scope. File: `leadership.md`.
