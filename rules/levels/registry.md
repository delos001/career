# Level Registry

**Used by:** cv-targeted, axis-classifier, inventory, narratives

Level values for axis classification and validation. Per-value framing rules in the same-folder files.

- **ic** - individual technical execution and depth in a domain or discipline; the core work is what the person builds, analyzes, or solves. File: `ic.md`.
- **leadership** - organizational leadership and the building or governance of capabilities, teams, and functions; people-management authority, organizational scope, or multi-function leadership. File: `leadership.md`.

Level is decided by the work the role describes. Job title and a JD's silence on direct reports do not decide it. When a role has both individual technical execution and leadership scope, leadership takes precedence. Owning a platform, capability, or standards for a whole function counts as organizational scope.
