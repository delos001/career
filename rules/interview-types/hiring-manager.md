# Interview type: Hiring Manager

## Identity
- The hiring manager owns the role and the team; the candidate's prospective direct manager.
- The round that probes role fit and substance most deeply, and typically gates the panel.
- Strongly bidirectional: they assess the candidate; the candidate assesses the real role (day-to-day, scope, whether the work matches the title).

## Emphasis
- Lead the prep into **The Role** (deepest role-understanding round: decision rights, real day-to-day, scope) and **Fit and Gaps** substance (an HM goes deeper than a screener).
- Talk craft peer-to-peer; the HM is technical and close to the work.
- The candidate's primary job in the room is often information-gathering, not selling.

## Research targets (for the research pass)
- **Interviewer intel is PRIMARY** - this is the prospective manager. One prep-research invocation. Degrade gracefully if the footprint is empty: confirm-live handling + an opener, no padded guesses.
- Team shape and reporting line (usually screen-sourced and unverified; mark confirm-live).

## Question Bank priorities
- Role-clarification anchors: day-to-day + hands-on / oversight split; decision rights (owns vs influences); first 6-12 months / what success looks like; level-vs-work; team and adjacent-role boundaries; roadmap.
- The opener that invites the interviewer's own path and framing (doubles as rapport and fills interviewer-intel gaps).

## Concerns to push here
- Role-scope and any "is this the job I think it is" concern - the HM is the best-positioned person to resolve these.
- Level-vs-work and day-to-day reality.
- Comp usually defers past this round.

## Posture
- Peer-level, substantive, collaborative. Go deep. The candidate is interviewing them too.

## Avoid
- Pitching in generalities or reciting the CV (the HM holds it).
- Treating HR / screen-sourced role claims as fact; confirm them with the HM.
- Letting the conversation collapse into pure logistics or tooling / vendor admin.
