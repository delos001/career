# Mature — CV Framing Rules

**Used by:** cv_targeted, role_evaluation

## Identity

Steady-state operation of established systems and processes. The deliverable is operational continuity, incremental improvement, governance, and risk management within a working operating model. Characteristic contexts: ongoing operation of an established function, optimization of well-understood processes, governance of stable platforms, risk management within mature regulated environments.

The work-state does not fit when the work is establishing new capability (greenfield), expanding scope (scaling), recovering from underperformance (turnaround), or merging/separating organizations (post-merger integration, divestiture).

## Achievement framing

Reads as mature signal: "optimized," "refined," "sustained," "governed," "stewarded," "tightened," "improved" (with quantified delta on an established baseline). Outcomes describe efficiency gains, risk reduction, quality improvement, or sustained performance against established benchmarks. Improvement is measured against a known prior state.

Reads as off-spec for mature: capability-creation framing, multipliers indicating scope expansion, recovery framing implying a prior failed state, integration framing implying multiple organizations.

## Adjacency

Translation signal for entries tagged with adjacent work-states:

- **scaling**: scaling work translates as mature only after the scaling endpoint is reached and operations enter steady state; in-flight scaling does not.
- **post-merger-integration**: post-merger work translates as mature once integration is complete and operations are steady; in-flight integration does not.
- **turnaround**: turnaround work translates as mature only after stabilization is achieved and operations are steady; in-flight recovery does not.
