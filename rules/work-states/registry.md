# Work-state Registry

**Used by:** cv-targeted, axis-classifier, inventory, narratives

Work-state values for axis classification and validation. Per-value framing rules in the same-folder files.

- **greenfield** - no prior structure, capability, or operating model existed; establishing rather than improving (standing up a new function, a first-of-its-kind capability, founding). File: `greenfield.md`.
- **scaling** - growing a functioning model to handle larger volume, scope, reach, or complexity; pilot to enterprise, harmonizing across sites, replicating across regions. File: `scaling.md`.
- **mature** - steady-state operation of established systems and processes; operational continuity, incremental improvement, governance, risk management within a working model. File: `mature.md`.
- **turnaround** - recovering a function, operation, or organization from chronic underperformance, structural failure, or sustained distress; restoring viability, stabilizing, rebuilding a failed model. File: `turnaround.md`.
- **post-merger-integration** - combining or harmonizing two organizations after a merger or acquisition; integrated operations, harmonized systems and processes, captured synergies. File: `post-merger-integration.md`.
- **divestiture** - separating an existing unit from a parent and establishing it as independent, or preparing it for sale; severed dependencies, standalone capability, operational independence. File: `divestiture.md`.
- **pivot** - redirecting a function, business model, or strategic focus while operations continue; changed direction without starting over. File: `pivot.md`.
