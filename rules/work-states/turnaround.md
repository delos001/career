# Turnaround — CV Framing Rules

**Used by:** cv_targeted, role_evaluation

## Identity

Work that recovers a function, operation, or organization from chronic underperformance, structural failure, or sustained distress. The deliverable is restoring viability, stabilizing operations, or rebuilding a failed model. Characteristic contexts: inheriting a failing function, stabilizing operations after sustained quality or performance issues, restructuring underperforming groups, rebuilding capability after attrition or organizational damage.

The work-state does not fit when underperformance is acute and event-driven rather than chronic (closer to crisis response, which is currently absorbed into turnaround), when the work is establishing new capability (greenfield), or when the work is steady-state operation of a healthy system (mature).

## Achievement framing

Reads as turnaround signal: "stabilized," "recovered," "restored," "restructured," "reset," "salvaged," "turned around." Outcomes describe recovery from a prior failed or distressed state. Before-and-after framing is particularly strong because the "before" describes a real failure (audit findings, performance gaps, attrition, missed milestones, regulatory action). The prior state must be specific and verifiable, not generic.

Reads as off-spec for turnaround: incremental-improvement framing on a healthy baseline (mature), capability-creation framing where no prior failed state existed (greenfield), framing that omits the prior distressed state (under-claims the recovery scope).

## Adjacency

Translation signal for entries tagged with adjacent work-states:

- **pivot**: pivot work translates as turnaround only when the redirection was driven by sustained underperformance rather than strategic choice; opportunistic pivots do not carry.
- **post-merger-integration**: post-merger work translates as turnaround when integration was distressed (failed cultural fit, missed synergies, value erosion); routine integration does not.
- **mature**: mature work translates as turnaround only when the candidate inherited the function in a distressed state and stabilized it before steady-state operation began; pure steady-state operation does not.
