# Post-Merger Integration — CV Framing Rules

**Used by:** cv_targeted, role_evaluation

## Identity

Work that combines or harmonizes two organizations after a merger or acquisition. The deliverable is integrated operations, harmonized systems and processes, captured synergies, and resolved redundancies. Characteristic contexts: integrating systems and platforms across acquired entities, harmonizing processes and governance, consolidating redundant functions, aligning cultures, capturing announced synergies.

The work-state does not fit when the work is separating organizations (divestiture), when it is steady-state operation of an already-integrated entity (mature), or when no merger or acquisition triggered the work.

## Achievement framing

Reads as post-merger integration signal: "integrated," "harmonized," "consolidated," "merged," "unified," "synchronized," "rationalized." Outcomes describe systems unified across legacy entities, processes harmonized across previously-distinct organizations, redundancies eliminated, synergies captured (cost or capability), governance aligned. Multi-legacy framing is the differentiator: the "before" is two distinct organizations, not one.

Reads as off-spec for post-merger integration: scope expansion within a single organization (scaling), efficiency gains within a single organization (mature), capability-creation where no prior counterpart existed (greenfield).

## Adjacency

Translation signal for entries tagged with adjacent work-states:

- **divestiture**: divestiture work translates partially because both involve organizational restructuring; the direction is opposite (separating vs combining), so framing must be reframed accordingly.
- **turnaround**: turnaround work translates as post-merger integration only when the integration itself was distressed; routine recovery does not.
- **mature**: mature work translates as post-merger integration only when the candidate's contribution was completing or sustaining the integration into steady-state; pre-merger or post-completion mature operation does not.
