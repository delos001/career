# Competency Registry

**Used by:** cv_targeted, role_evaluation, experience_inventory

Activity-level competency values for the entry-level `Competency:` tag in `Experience_Inventory.md`. Multi-value, pipe-delimited. Lowercase kebab format. Industry- and specialty-agnostic by design: a competency names a unit of work that means the same thing across contexts (managing a budget is the same competency for a clinical PM and a small-business owner). Industry context is captured by the Industry axis; specialty context by the Specialty axis. New tag values must validate against this list; expansion is a deliberate edit to this file.

Activity-level redesign 2026-05: replaced the prior inventory-derived 31-term registry with a 36-term top-down activity taxonomy. Inventory artifacts (`temp/competency_extraction.md`, `temp/competency_clustering_proposal.md`) retained as evidence trail of activities present in user inventory.

## People & Team

- **people-management**: direct management of staff and team operations.
- **mentoring**: coaching, peer technical guidance, individual development.
- **talent-acquisition**: hiring, recruiting, candidate sourcing and selection.
- **performance-management**: performance reviews, feedback, development planning.

## Training & Knowledge

- **training-delivery**: delivering training sessions, instructor-led training.
- **curriculum-development**: training content creation, curriculum and instructional design.
- **knowledge-transfer**: knowledge sharing, peer-to-peer skill transfer, onboarding others.

## Communication & Influence

- **stakeholder-management**: managing relationships with sponsors, partners, internal and external stakeholders.
- **executive-communication**: communication with senior leadership, executive briefings.
- **cross-functional-collaboration**: working across functions and teams.
- **change-management**: enterprise change leadership, adoption strategy, resistance management.

## Project & Operations

- **project-management**: end-to-end project delivery, planning, execution, closure.
- **timeline-management**: schedule planning, milestone tracking, deadline accountability.
- **budget-management**: budget planning, financial oversight, cost optimization.
- **vendor-management**: vendor and partner oversight, selection, contract relationships.
- **risk-management**: risk identification, assessment, mitigation, escalation.
- **operations-management**: ongoing operational oversight of business or technical operations.

## Strategy & Org

- **strategic-planning**: strategy development, capability planning, vision and roadmap setting.
- **roadmap-development**: capability or product roadmap authoring, future-state design.
- **organizational-design**: org structure design, operating model design, function building.
- **process-design-and-improvement**: process design, improvement, re-engineering, workflow design.

## Data & Analytics

- **data-analysis-and-statistics**: quantitative and qualitative analysis, statistical methods, hypothesis testing, EDA.
- **data-engineering**: ETL/ELT, data pipelines, data transformation, database and schema design.
- **reporting-and-dashboards**: reporting model development, BI dashboards, data visualization.
- **programming**: scripting and code development across languages.

## Technology Build

- **application-development**: custom application build, software development, internal tooling.
- **ai-ml-development**: AI/ML system design and development, model deployment, AI agents.
- **technology-evaluation**: platform evaluation, technology selection, vendor and tool assessment.
- **system-implementation-and-configuration**: deployment, setup, and configuration of platforms and systems.

## Standards & Compliance

- **standards-and-specification-development**: authoring data, technical, or process standards and specifications.
- **standards-application**: applying existing standards and specifications in operational work.
- **procedure-authoring**: SOP and controlled-procedure authoring.
- **regulatory-compliance**: applying regulatory frameworks and ensuring compliance.
- **audit-and-inspection-response**: audit preparation, response, findings management, inspection readiness.
- **quality-management**: quality oversight, quality assurance, quality monitoring.

## Governance

- **governance**: governance framework, accountability, decision-rights, oversight body design.
