# CV Sections - Which Sections Appear

**Used by:** cv-targeted.

**Purpose.** This file holds the rules a skill follows to decide which sections appear on the CV for this job, from the inventory, `jd_needs.md` and the retrieval manifest. It decides only whether each section is included. Section order and layout are in `cv-structure.md`; what content goes into each included section is in `cv-section-content.md`; how it is worded is in `cv-writing.md`.

## PLAN: Required sections

The sections marked `(required)` in the `cv-structure.md` Section order block always appear.

## PLAN: Optional sections

Decide for each job; no section appears because it usually does. For each optional section below, include it only if all three tests hold. (1) Its inventory section has entries (not `Entries: None`). (2) The entries are relevant to this job. (3) The section more effectively persuades a reviewer to interview the candidate than whatever would be cut to make room for it. Record each decision in `drafting_plan.md`. Independent Projects is the exception to (2) and (3) (see Independent Projects below).

```
Inventory section -> CV section
Employment & Role History -> Professional Experience, Earlier Professional Roles
Experience Entries -> Professional Experience
Independent & Volunteer Projects -> Independent Projects
Industry Exposure Profile -> none of its own; may supply terms to Core Competencies and the Summary
Technical Experience -> Technical Proficiencies
Education -> Education
Professional Training -> Certifications & Training
Professional Certifications -> Certifications & Training
Professional Affiliations -> Professional Affiliations
Publications -> not designed; no entries yet
Presentations -> not designed; no entries yet
Awards & Honors -> not designed; no entries yet
Languages -> Languages
```

A section marked not designed gets its rules when its first entry exists (`design/deferrals.md`, `cv-rules-for-empty-inventory-sections`).

## PLAN: Earlier Professional Roles

The section appears when at least one role is placed there under "Which roles appear in Professional Experience" in `cv-section-content.md`.

## PLAN: Independent Projects

The section appears whenever the inventory has a current project, one whose End Date is blank. A project that is not current appears only when it passes the three tests in Optional sections.

## PLAN: Professional Affiliations

Include only for board, governance, or senior-leadership jobs where the JD mentions memberships, board seats, or committee roles; omit otherwise.

## PLAN: Languages

Include when the job names a language or works across regions where one matters; omit a candidate's native language alone.

## PLAN: Technical Proficiencies

For pure-leadership jobs the section may be omitted when the JD does not ask for technical tools.
