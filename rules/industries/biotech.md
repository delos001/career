# Biotech - CV Framing Rules

**Used by:** cv-targeted, axis-classifier

## Vocabulary

Modality terms: biologics, large-molecule, monoclonal antibody (mAb), antibody-drug conjugate (ADC), bispecific antibody, fusion protein, recombinant protein, vaccine (mRNA, viral vector, subunit, conjugate), oligonucleotide therapy (ASO, siRNA), peptide therapeutic, lipid nanoparticle (LNP) delivery, self-amplifying mRNA (saRNA).

Cell and gene therapy terms: gene therapy, cell therapy, CAR-T, TCR-T, TIL, ex vivo, in vivo, AAV, LV (lentivirus), genome editing (CRISPR/Cas9, base editing, prime editing - "genome editing" preferred over "gene editing" per FDA finalized guidance 2024-2025), iPSC (induced pluripotent stem cell), MSC (mesenchymal stromal cell - "stromal" preferred over "stem" per ISCT May 2025 nomenclature), autologous, allogeneic, chain of identity (COI), chain of custody (CoC), patient-specific manufacturing, apheresis center, cryogenic logistics.

Trial-lifecycle terms: study, trial, protocol, site, investigator (PI, sub-I), participant, patient, Phase I/II/III/IV, FIH, pivotal, registrational, arm, cohort, randomization, blinding (single-blind, double-blind, open-label), placebo-controlled, endpoint, screening, enrollment, indication, therapeutic area. Biotech-emphasis additions: pre-IND meetings, IND-enabling studies, first-in-human (FIH), proof of concept (PoC), dose escalation, expansion cohort, basket trial, umbrella trial.

Participant terminology: "participant" is the current preference in protocols and consent forms (TransCelerate Common Protocol Template, NIH/FDA templates, ClinicalTrials.gov, NEJM editorial standard); "patient" remains acceptable when the trial population is patients; "subject" is dated.

Submission terms: regulatory submission, pre-IND, IND, NDA, BLA, sBLA, ANDA, sNDA, MAA, 505(b)(2), CTD, eCTD (v3.2.2 current; v4.0 supported), DMF, accelerated approval, breakthrough therapy designation (BTD), regenerative medicine advanced therapy (RMAT), fast track, orphan drug designation, priority review, START Program (CBER program supporting rare-disease CGT trials, initiated 2024), PRIME (EMA priority medicines), conditional marketing authorization (EU), approval under exceptional circumstances (EU ATMP-specific).

Bioprocess and manufacturing terms: upstream processing, downstream processing, cell culture, fermentation, perfusion, bioreactor, single-use bioreactor (SUB), fill/finish, lyophilization, viral inactivation, viral clearance, drug substance (DS), drug product (DP), comparability study, comparability protocol, host cell protein (HCP), critical quality attribute (CQA), bioassay, potency assay, cell bank (MCB, WCB), stable cell line, transient transfection, process analytical technology (PAT), continuous manufacturing, platform manufacturing.

Regulatory frameworks: GxP family (GCP, GMP, GLP, GDP, GVP), ICH (E6(R3) is current GCP standard, finalized Jan 2025; E2 family for PV; E8(R1), E9, M4, M11), 21 CFR Part 11, EU Annex 11, EU CTR (536/2014). Biologics-specific additions: ICH Q5A(R2) (adopted FDA Jan 2024, EMA Jun 2024 - covers viral vectors, NGS/PCR assays, continuous manufacturing, platform/modular validation), Q5B genetic stability, Q5C stability (currently revising per ICH), Q5D cell substrate, Q5E comparability, Q6B biotech specifications, Q11 DS development, Q12 lifecycle management, Q13 continuous manufacturing, Q14 analytical method development, Q2(R2) analytical method validation. 21 CFR Parts 600-680 (biologics), 21 CFR Part 1271 (HCT/P), CBER guidance suite for cell and gene therapy.

Regulatory bodies: CBER (Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research) - primary for cell/gene therapy, blood/blood products, allergenics, live biotherapeutics, HCT/Ps, vaccines; OTP (Office of Therapeutic Products - CBER super-office handling >2,500 active CGT INDs as of 2024; replaced OTAT). CDER for therapeutic proteins post-2020 BPCI Act transition: monoclonal antibodies, fusion proteins, insulin, growth factors, cytokines, immunomodulators, products altering hematopoiesis. EMA CAT (Committee for Advanced Therapies for ATMPs), MHRA, PMDA.

Stakeholders: sponsor, CRO, FSP, FSO, vendor, site, IRB/IEC, DSMB/DMC, KOL, HCP. Biotech-emphasis additions: platform partner, manufacturing partner, CDMO, academic collaborator, patient advocacy organization, apheresis center, cryogenic logistics provider.

Operating concepts: modality, indication expansion, lead indication, pipeline, IND-enabling, IND-cleared, clinical-stage, preclinical, discovery-stage, translational research, long-term follow-up (LTFU - currently 15-year requirement for gene therapy under regulatory reassessment), CMC flexibility (post-FDA Jan 2026 announcement on flexible CMC for CGTs).

Non-preferred terms: "drug" without modality qualifier (reads as small-molecule-default); "gene editing" (use "genome editing"); "mesenchymal stem cell" (use "mesenchymal stromal cell"); "OTAT" (use "OTP"); "subject" (use "participant"); generic tech-industry verbs ("ship", "iterate") in operational descriptions.

## Emphasis

Hiring-panel emphasis falls on: platform-vs-asset framing; multi-hat scope (biotech roles span more functions per role than pharma equivalents); speed and pivot capability under uncertainty; IND-enabling and FIH study experience (biotech weighting tilts earlier in development than large pharma); regulatory-designation literacy (BTD, RMAT, Fast Track, Orphan, Accelerated Approval - note: FDA Sept 2025 draft "Expedited Programs for Regenerative Medicine Therapies" supersedes Feb 2019 RMAT guidance, introducing RWE/external controls for designation and formalizing long-term safety monitoring expectation; entries phrased only to Feb 2019 framing read as not current); biologics CMC literacy (Q5 series with Q5A(R2) updates, Q12-Q14, comparability, host-cell impurity, bioprocess); modality-specific manufacturing experience (cell therapy COI/CoC, gene therapy viral vector, ADC bioconjugation, mRNA/LNP CMC, genome editing CMC).

Capital-stage signal reads through CV: discovery-stage, IND-enabling, IND-cleared, FIH, Phase I dose escalation, PoC, pivotal Phase III, BLA submission, post-approval commercial; "pivotal-ready" and "BLA-ready" are current late-stage signals. Biotech panels read trajectory across stages.

Evidence over claims: biotech panels weight documented program advancement (IND filings, FIH dosing, milestones met, designations earned, partnerships closed, comparability studies completed per Q5E, viral clearance validation packages per Q5A(R2), CMC flexibilities negotiated with FDA) over adjectival framing.

Hiring-panel de-emphasis falls on: large-pharma silo-specialist framing without cross-functional context; commercial-scale operational metrics (volume, throughput) when role context is clinical-stage; pure research-scientist framing without build-and-scale context (CGT sector maturing into manufacturing/commercial phase weights operator credentials).

## Adjacency

Translation signal for entries tagged with adjacent industries:

- **pharma**: clinical, regulatory, GxP context translates directly. Biotech work scales down operationally (smaller programs, multi-hat); pharma work scales up to commercial readiness. BLA-side regulatory experience translates as biologics literacy. Small-molecule CMC (ICH Q1, Q3, Q6A) does not transfer; Q5 series, Q6B, Q11, Q12, Q13, Q14 are the relevant cross-references.
- **cro**: sponsor-side perspective is the natural bridge; sponsor oversight of biotech-specific outsourced work (CDMO bioprocess, cell-therapy manufacturing) is the alternative framing. Specialty CGT CROs and cell-therapy CDMOs are a distinct sub-segment.
- **med-device**: regulatory rigor translates; combination-product (drug-device combo) and companion-diagnostic (CDx) experience is the meaningful crossover. Delivery devices for gene therapy (e.g., AAV intrathecal/intraocular delivery) are a combination-product signal.
- **diagnostics**: companion diagnostic (CDx) co-development experience translates; assay validation, IVDR/LDT context is the relevant signal. FDA LDT rule transitions ongoing through 2028; current hiring contexts treat IVDR as the more substantive signal.
- **eclinical**: vendor-management of cell-therapy COI/CoC orchestration platforms (TrakCel, Vineti-class systems) is the meaningful biotech-specific eClinical signal; GTP-aware EDC remains relevant.
- **generics**: CMC discipline, eCTD submission mechanics, CGMP fundamentals, and PAI / 483 / Warning Letter inspection-readiness experience carry as regulated-submission rigor. Small-molecule synthesis, dissolution-based BE, and 505(j) ANDA pathway specifics do not - biologics use 351(a) BLA or 351(k) biosimilar. 505(b)(2) work on a peptide or complex molecule, or combination-product generic experience (prefilled syringe, auto-injector), is a stronger bridge than pure small-molecule ANDA work; acknowledge the modality gap explicitly.
