# Pharma — CV Framing Rules

**Used by:** cv_targeted, role_evaluation

## Vocabulary

Trial-lifecycle terms: study, trial, protocol, site, investigator, patient, subject (both acceptable; "patient" trends preferred post-2020), Phase I/II/III/IV, pivotal, registrational.

Submission terms: regulatory submission, pre-IND, IND, NDA, BLA, ANDA, CTD.

Regulatory frameworks: GxP family (GCP, GMP, GLP, GDP, GVP), ICH, ICH E6, 21 CFR Part 11, EU Annex 11.

Regulatory bodies: FDA, EMA, MHRA, PMDA.

Sector-wide stakeholders: sponsor, CRO, vendor.

Non-preferred terms: "customer" for trial subjects, "user" for clinicians, generic tech-industry verbs ("ship", "iterate") in operational descriptions.

## Dialect

The industry is acronym-dense. Acronyms recognized without expansion in pharma hiring contexts: FDA, EMA, MHRA, PMDA, GxP, GCP, GMP, GLP, GVP, ICH, IRB, IND, NDA, BLA, ANDA, CTD, CRO, EDC, CTMS, eTMF, IRT.

## Emphasis

Hiring-panel emphasis falls on: regulated-environment work; multi-function integration across clinical, regulatory, quality, and CMC; external-partner ecosystem (CRO, FSP, vendor); therapeutic-area depth; trial-phase exposure (Phase I, II, III, post-marketing); submission-era exposure (pre-IND, pivotal, registrational).

Regulated work paired with its governing framework (GCP-compliant, 21 CFR Part 11-aligned, ICH E6(R3)) reads as regulatory literacy. Regulatory presence without framework attribution does not.

Hiring-panel de-emphasis falls on: internal-tool building unattached to clinical or regulatory output; technology delivery without clinical or quality outcome.

## Adjacency

Translation signal for entries tagged with adjacent industries:

- **biotech**: framing maps directly; regulatory and trial-phase context carries.
- **cro**: sponsor-side perspective is the natural bridge; cross-sponsor program oversight is the alternative framing.
- **generics**: ANDA/regulatory and CMC are the relevant signal; clinical novelty is not.
- **med-device**: 510(k)/PMA experience translates as regulatory rigor; device-specific terminology does not carry.
- **diagnostics**: regulated-environment and quality-systems work translates; assay-specific detail does not.
