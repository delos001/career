# CRO - CV Framing Rules

**Used by:** cv_targeted, axis-classifier

## Vocabulary

Service-model terms: full-service outsourcing (FSO), functional service provider/provision (FSP), hybrid FSP/FSO (first-class delivery model 2024-2026; sponsors mix both across portfolios per Applied Clinical Trials, pharmaphorum, The Medicine Maker). Newer named variants: Flexible FSP (Novotech), FSP 2.0 / FSP+ (FSP layered with consulting and analytics), blended FSP, flex-resourcing. Strategic partnership, preferred provider, co-development partner - labels for the deepest sponsor relationships (CRO Industry Outlook 2026 frames CROs as "strategic co-developers, not transactional vendors").

Contract and commercial terms: master services agreement (MSA), statement of work (SOW) / work order, change order, time and materials (T&M), fixed-price / firm-fixed-price, unit-based pricing, milestone-based pricing, full-time equivalent (FTE), pass-through cost, scope creep, scope management, budget reconciliation, project profitability, clinical trial agreement (CTA - sponsor-CRO instrument; under FDA Sep 2024 DCT guidance must explicitly identify decentralized-element ownership), transfer of regulatory obligations (TORO - ICH E6(R3) Section 3.9 concept). Commercial-side: backlog, book-to-bill, burn rate, revenue recognition, net new awards, cancellations, win rate, bid-defense conversion.

Service-line terms: clinical operations, clinical monitoring (SQV/SIV/IMV/COV - site qualification, initiation, interim monitoring, close-out visits), risk-based monitoring (RBM), risk-based quality management (RBQM - R3-aligned umbrella), centralized monitoring, remote monitoring, source data verification (SDV), source data review (SDR), data management (DM), biostatistics, statistical programming (SAS, increasingly R), medical writing, regulatory affairs / regulatory operations, pharmacovigilance / drug safety, QA / GCP audit, RTSM (Randomization and Trial Supply Management - current term; IRT/IWRS dated but recognized), central lab, bioanalytical, eClinical (EDC, CTMS, eTMF, eCOA, ePRO), real-world evidence (RWE) services, patient recruitment / site identification & feasibility, medical affairs / medical monitoring. Emerging 2025-2026 roles: AI Systems Coordinator, Clinical Informatics Manager, Automation Validation Expert.

Stakeholders: sponsor, CRO, FSP, FSO, vendor, site, IRB/IEC, DSMB/DMC, KOL, HCP. CRO-emphasis additions: sponsor (the CRO's customer - primary external stakeholder), business development (BD), capture management, proposal / bid defense, account management, strategic account director / strategic partnership lead, project director, project manager, study lead, lead CRA, client services, alliance management, functional service head, functional lead, therapeutic area head.

Trial-lifecycle terms: study, trial, protocol, site, investigator (PI, sub-I), participant, patient, Phase I/II/III/IV, FIH, pivotal, registrational, arm, cohort, randomization, blinding (single-blind, double-blind, open-label), placebo-controlled, endpoint, screening, enrollment, indication, therapeutic area. CRO-emphasis: trial conduct against contracted deliverables, sponsor-defined endpoints, sponsor SOP compliance (in addition to CRO SOPs), sponsor-mandated systems, joint review meetings, cycle-time metrics (FPI, LPI, LPLV, DBL-to-CSR), enrollment-vs-plan, site activation rate, monitoring visit compliance.

Participant terminology: "participant" is the current preference in protocols and consent forms (TransCelerate Common Protocol Template, NIH/FDA templates, ClinicalTrials.gov, NEJM editorial standard); "patient" remains acceptable when the trial population is patients; "subject" is dated.

Submission terms: regulatory submission, pre-IND, IND, NDA, BLA, sBLA, ANDA, sNDA, MAA, 505(b)(2), CTD, eCTD (v3.2.2 current; v4.0 supported), DMF.

Regulatory frameworks: GxP family (GCP, GMP, GLP, GDP, GVP), ICH (E6(R3) is current GCP standard, finalized Jan 2025; E2 family for PV; E8(R1), E9, M4, M11), 21 CFR Part 11, EU Annex 11, EU CTR (536/2014). CRO-emphasis: ICH E6(R3) Section 3.9 (sponsor oversight of CRO and transferred trial-related duties and functions; EU adopted Jul 2025, FDA Sep 2025) - replaces R2 Section 5.2 vocabulary. Key R3 phrasing: "service provider" (broader term covering CROs and other vendors), "sponsor oversight" (explicit, non-delegable accountability), "fit-for-purpose," "risk-proportionate." ICH E8(R1) sponsor-CRO operating model with quality-by-design and critical-to-quality (CtQ) factors. FDA Sep 2024 DCT final guidance (Conducting Clinical Trials with Decentralized Elements - sponsor oversight cannot be outsourced; CTA specifies decentralized-element ownership). CTIS for EU-trial CRO submissions.

Operating concepts: cross-sponsor program oversight; contract delivery against milestones and SLAs; resource utilization and billable hours; sponsor escalation; multi-protocol portfolio management; multi-therapeutic-area capability; audit-readiness across multiple sponsor quality systems; risk-based quality management (RBQM) per E6(R3); critical-to-quality (CtQ) factors; quality tolerance limits (QTL); key risk indicators (KRI); centralized statistical monitoring (CSM); oversight plan; quality management plan (QMP); joint operating committee (JOC); joint steering committee (JSC); service-level agreement (SLA); key performance indicator (KPI); RACI; transition (study migration between CROs or in-housing).

Non-preferred terms: "client" instead of "sponsor" in operational CV descriptions where "sponsor" is standard; "subject" (use "participant" - TransCelerate/NIH/NEJM standard, applies on CRO side too); "IWRS" alone (pair with or replace by "RTSM"); ICH E6(R2) Section 5.2 phrasing as live framework (R3 supersedes); generic agency/consultancy verbs ("delivered for the client," "drove engagement") without trial-operational specifics.

## Emphasis

Hiring-panel emphasis falls on: cross-sponsor portfolio breadth (number of sponsors served, range of therapeutic areas and trial phases, ability to context-switch across sponsor SOPs - breadth across multiple sponsors in the same TA reads stronger than depth in a single pharma program); contract delivery and SLA achievement (on-time milestone delivery, scope-vs-actuals, change-order management, utilization rate, billable-hours ratio for FSP, study budget under management, backlog/revenue contribution); P&L and account growth at Project Director and above; BD/Strategic Account roles weight win rate, bid-defense conversion, account growth, MSA-to-SOW ratio, net new awards; ICH E6(R3) Section 3.9 sponsor-oversight literacy from the receiving (CRO) side (phrasing entries against R3 Section 3.9 rather than legacy R2 Section 5.2 signals current literacy); FSP delivery experience (rising signal - 41% of sponsors increased FSP usage 2023-2024 vs 27% FSO; hybrid FSP/FSO grew 22% to 34% in small/mid-size sponsors); hybrid model navigation (operating across FSP and FSO scopes for the same sponsor); DCT/hybrid-trial operational experience post FDA Sep 2024 final guidance; AI/automation literacy (agentic AI for trial monitoring, AI-assisted protocol design, automated risk detection - >70% of CROs deploying AI analytics by 2026; validation, model oversight, and GCP-aligned AI governance are the framing that translates).

Therapeutic-area framing depends on role: monitoring and operations roles weight TA breadth; specialist roles (medical writing, biostatistics) weight TA depth.

Evidence over claims: CRO panels weight documented sponsor outcomes (deliverables on time, change orders managed without delivery erosion, audits passed, sponsor renewal/expansion, profitability against bid) over adjectival framing.

Hiring-panel de-emphasis falls on: internal-only pharma sponsor experience without external-vendor-management exposure (single-sponsor career history is a flag for portfolio-context roles); tech-build framing without trial-operational outcome; heavy FSO-only background without FSP exposure (noted gap given the sponsor-preference shift); commercial-launch decision-making; sponsor-internal portfolio decisions (CRO does not own product).

## Adjacency

Translation signal for entries tagged with adjacent industries:

- **pharma**: cross-sponsor portfolio reframes as cross-program oversight; account/SOW management reframes as vendor/contract management; sponsor-oversight-under-R3 vocabulary translates directly. Service-side perspective on the same sponsor-CRO interface pharma owns from the other side.
- **biotech**: CRO experience translates strongly - small biotech is the heaviest FSO outsourcer and reads CRO experience as direct operational extension. Frame around "operated as the biotech's clinical operations function" rather than vendor framing.
- **med-device**: CRO med-device service lines exist (510(k)/PMA/IDE support); ISO 13485 / 21 CFR 820 / IDE replace IND framing. CRO-side trial-monitoring discipline carries; device-engineering specificity does not.
- **eclinical**: CRO operations experience is strong adjacency - CRO staff are primary users and implementation partners of eClinical platforms. Frame around vendor selection, validation, integration, and sponsor-SOP-aligned configuration. Veeva Vault, Medidata Rave, Oracle Clinical One, and Florence are current dominant platforms.
- **diagnostics**: CRO central-lab and bioanalytical experience is direct adjacency; LDT/IVDR and assay-validation context replaces broader trial-conduct framing.
- **generics**: sponsor-side oversight of outsourced BE studies (single-dose PK in healthy volunteers, fasted/fed crossover, RSABE for HVDs, bioanalytical method validation) translates as CRO clin-pharm and bioanalytical service-line experience; GCP, ICH E6(R3), and CDISC SDTM submission discipline carry directly. Specialty BE/clin-pharm CROs are a distinct sub-segment with thin-margin, high-throughput economics. Patient-facing Phase 2/3 site management and therapeutic-area medical monitoring do not carry; frame as healthy-volunteer Phase 1 / BE operational depth rather than late-phase trial conduct.
