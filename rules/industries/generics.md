# Generics - CV Framing Rules

**Used by:** cv_targeted, axis-classifier

## Vocabulary

Submission pathway terms: ANDA (505(j)), 505(b)(2) NDA, Reference Listed Drug (RLD), Reference Standard (RS), Orange Book, therapeutic equivalence (TE) codes (AB, AA, BX), paragraph I/II/III/IV certifications, section viii statement (skinny label), 180-day exclusivity, 30-month stay, first-to-file, Suitability Petition, REMS.

Equivalence and scientific terms: pharmaceutical equivalence, bioequivalence (BE), bioavailability (BA), Cmax, AUC, Tmax, geometric mean ratio (GMR), 90% confidence interval (80.00-125.00% acceptance window per 21 CFR Part 320), narrow therapeutic index (NTI; tightened acceptance ~90.00-111.11%), highly variable drug (HVD), reference-scaled average bioequivalence (RSABE), Biopharmaceutics Classification System (BCS), BCS-based biowaiver, dissolution profile (f1/f2 similarity factor), in vivo PK, in vitro release/permeation testing (IVRT/IVPT), IVIVC.

CMC and quality terms: active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), key starting material (KSM), finished dosage form (FDF), excipient, Quality Target Product Profile (QTPP), Critical Quality Attribute (CQA), Critical Process Parameter (CPP), Critical Material Attribute (CMA), design space, control strategy, process performance qualification (PPQ), stability, container closure, impurity profile (ICH Q3A/B), elemental impurities (ICH Q3D), nitrosamine risk assessment.

Submission and interaction terms: eCTD Module 1/2/3/4/5, Quality Overall Summary (QOS), Drug Master File (DMF; Type II for APIs), Letter of Authorization (LOA), Completeness Assessment (CA), Refuse-to-Receive (RTR), Information Request (IR), Discipline Review Letter (DRL), Complete Response Letter (CRL), Goal Date, Controlled Correspondence (CC), Pre-ANDA Product Development / Pre-Submission / Mid-Review Cycle meetings, Product-Specific Guidance (PSG).

Manufacturing and inspection terms: CGMP (21 CFR Parts 210 and 211), ICH Q7 (API CGMP), Q8(R2)/Q9/Q10/Q11 (QbD foundation), Form FDA 483, Establishment Inspection Report (EIR), Warning Letter, Import Alert, Consent Decree, classification outcomes (NAI / VAI / OAI), Pre-Approval Inspection (PAI), data integrity (ALCOA+ / ALCOA++), audit trail review, CMO, CDMO.

Commercial and IP terms: authorized generic (AG), first generic, branded generic, Paragraph IV litigation, launch-at-risk, skinny label / carve-out, GSK v. Teva (2026 Supreme Court; reshaping induced-infringement risk on section viii carve-outs), loss of exclusivity (LOE), Patent Trial and Appeal Board (PTAB) inter partes review.

Counterpart bodies and frameworks: FDA Office of Generic Drugs (OGD) within CDER (distinct from OND that brand pharma faces); GDUFA III (reauthorized Sept 2022 through Sept 2027; FY2026 fees effective Oct 1 2025; 90% of standard original ANDAs to be reviewed in 10 months); EMA Article 10(1)/10(3) (8-year reference-product authorization window); MHRA, Health Canada, PMDA, TGA, ANVISA, NMPA; WHO Prequalification (PQ) for procurement by UN agencies and many LMIC markets.

Data standards: CDISC SDTM (SDTMv2.0 and SDTMIG v3.4 required for new ANDA-submitted clinical study data per FDA Data Standards Catalog; v3.2 support ended), ADaM, Define-XML, eCTD as the submission envelope.

Non-preferred terms: "drug discovery" or "develop a new drug" for generics work (the pathway starts from a known RLD); "clinical trial in patients" for routine BE studies (most are single-dose PK in healthy volunteers); "generic biologic" (biologics use 351(k) biosimilar pathway, not ANDA); "copycat" / "knock-off" (pejorative; FDA framing is "therapeutically equivalent"); "subjects" (dated vs "participants" / "healthy volunteers" in newer guidance); generic tech-industry verbs ("disrupt", "move fast") in CGMP contexts.

## Dialect

The industry is acronym-dense and deeply pathway-aware. Acronyms recognized without expansion in generics hiring contexts: FDA, OGD, CDER, OND, EMA, MHRA, PMDA, TGA, ANVISA, NMPA, WHO PQ, LMIC, ICH, GDUFA, CFR, CGMP, GMP, GCP, QbD, CMC, QTPP, CQA, CPP, CMA, API, KSM, FDF, CMO, CDMO, CRO, DMF, LOA, CA, CC, eCTD, ESG, QOS, PSG, ANDA, NDA, BLA, RLD, RS, TE, BE, BA, PK, AUC, Cmax, Tmax, GMR, NTI, HVD, BCS, RSABE, IVIVC, IVRT, IVPT, PAI, EIR, OAI, VAI, NAI, RTR, IR, DRL, CRL, REMS, AG, LOE, IP, COGS, MSL, PTAB, SDTM, SDTMIG, ADaM, CDISC, ALCOA, PPQ, QMS, MDI, DPI, IVD, LDT, CLIA, CAP, IVDR, ISO, IEC, SaMD, UDI, HPLC, LC-MS, EDC, CTMS, CRF, CSV, GAMP, eTMF, ePRO, eCOA, IRT, RTSM.

## Emphasis

Hiring-panel emphasis falls on: ANDA and 505(b)(2) submission throughput and approval record (filings, approvals, first-cycle vs CRL cycles, named RLDs reverse-engineered, paragraph IV experience with outcomes); bioequivalence study design judgment (fasted vs fed, single-dose vs multi-dose, replicate vs 2x2 crossover, RSABE for HVDs, in vitro pathways per the relevant PSG); CMC and QbD depth (QTPP/CQA/CPP work, dissolution method development, impurity strategy including nitrosamine risk assessment, stability program design); inspection readiness and remediation (PAI hosting with NAI/VAI/OAI outcomes, 483 response, Warning Letter closure, data-integrity remediation under ALCOA+); DMF strategy and supplier qualification (multi-source API strategies, KSM sourcing including China-dependence risk, Completeness Assessment management, LOA portfolio); IP and exclusivity strategy (paragraph IV timing, 180-day exclusivity capture, section viii carve-out judgment under post-GSK-v.-Teva risk, Orange Book monitoring, launch-at-risk decision support); complex-generic capability (inhalation, ophthalmic, topical, peptide, drug-device combination products where PSGs are evolving and entry barriers are high); multi-jurisdiction filing experience (parallel FDA / EMA / Health Canada / MHRA / PMDA / ANVISA / WHO PQ with eCTD competency); cost-of-goods discipline (API contract negotiation, yield, per-tablet cost reduction at thin margins).

Regulated work paired with its governing artifact (specific Module 3 section, specific PSG, specific dissolution method, specific CRL deficiency resolved) reads as generics literacy. Generic verbs without dossier-level specificity ("supported regulatory submissions," "ensured GMP compliance," "managed quality systems") read as filler.

Evidence over claims: generics panels weight ANDA / 505(b)(2) numbers and approval dates, named RLDs, paragraph IV outcomes, PSG-aligned BE study designs, DMF count and CA pass rates, 483 observation counts and closure timelines, PAI outcomes, launch dates relative to LOE, specific COGS or yield improvements over adjectival framing.

Hiring-panel de-emphasis falls on: de novo discovery, target validation, and lead optimization vocabulary (off-axis for generics); brand-NDA Phase 1/2/3 sponsor framing applied to BE work (pathway confusion); novel-mechanism positioning and KOL strategy (generics compete on price, substitutability, and supply reliability, not promotion); "patient journey" or commercial-differentiation messaging; high-touch field medical and MSL work.

## Adjacency

Translation signal for entries tagged with adjacent industries:

- **pharma**: core CMC vocabulary (eCTD Module 3, ICH Q-series, CGMP under 21 CFR 210/211), FDA submission mechanics, OGD/CDER counterpart awareness, PAI / 483 / Warning Letter experience, stability and analytical method development carry directly. Clinical development of novel molecules, medical affairs, and NDA-scale safety database stewardship do not. Frame as "regulated CMC, BE, and submission rigor against tight GDUFA timelines"; map specific artifacts (Module 3 sections, PAI outcomes) to the pharma equivalent.
- **biotech**: CMC discipline, quality systems vocabulary, CGMP fundamentals, eCTD submissions, and inspection-readiness experience are broadly transferable. Small-molecule synthesis and dissolution/PK-based BE pathways do not carry (biologics use 351(a) BLA or 351(k) biosimilar, not 505(j)). Lead with quality systems and regulated-submission experience; acknowledge the modality gap explicitly. 505(b)(2) work on a complex molecule or peptide is a stronger biotech bridge than pure small-molecule ANDA work.
- **cro**: bioequivalence study design, clinical pharmacology, bioanalytical method development and validation, PK modeling, healthy-volunteer study operations, sponsor-CRO oversight, GCP, ICH E6(R3), and CDISC SDTM submissions carry; most generic sponsors outsource BE studies to specialized CROs, so generics regulatory and clin-pharm roles routinely oversee CRO work. Patient-facing Phase 2/3 site management, therapeutic-area medical monitoring, and large-scale efficacy biostatistics do not.
- **med-device**: quality systems mindset (CGMP / 21 CFR 820 share QMS DNA), inspection readiness, design controls for drug-device combination generics (MDIs, DPIs, auto-injectors, transdermal patches, prefilled syringes), human factors and usability for device-similarity assessment, and supplier qualification carry. 510(k) / De Novo / PMA pathways, ISO 13485, IEC 60601, SaMD, and UDI do not. Combination-product generic experience is the strongest bridge.
- **eclinical**: CDISC SDTM (and ADaM) literacy for BE study submissions, eCTD via FDA ESG, EDC for BE study CRFs, audit-trail review, and ALCOA+ data integrity carry. Trial-platform design (EDC architecture, CTMS, eTMF, ePRO/eCOA, IRT/RTSM), CSV under GAMP 5 from the vendor side, and large-trial data architecture do not. Position as a regulated-data consumer with deep ALCOA+ / Part 11 / CDISC fluency from the sponsor side.
- **diagnostics**: shared themes are limited to general FDA literacy, quality-system principles, and analytical method development discipline (HPLC, LC-MS, dissolution, impurity analysis transfer rigor to assay validation, but the regulatory framework differs). IVD pathways (510(k), De Novo, PMA for diagnostics; LDT framework), CLIA, CAP, ISO 15189, analytical and clinical validation of diagnostic assays, companion diagnostic co-development, and IVDR do not carry. Bridge is narrow; lead with regulated-industry rigor without overclaiming diagnostics-pathway expertise.
