# Orientation Registry

**Used by:** cv-targeted, axis-classifier, inventory, narratives

Orientation values for axis classification and validation. Per-value framing rules in the same-folder files.

- **transformation-strategy** - delivery of organizational capability or structural change; capability built, operating model redesigned and adopted, change program led at scale, turnaround by rebuilding. File: `transformation-strategy.md`.
- **data-analytics** - delivery of data-driven insight or governed analytical capability; decisions improved by analysis, analytics functions built or governed, data quality/acquisition frameworks as decision infrastructure. File: `data-analytics.md`.
- **process-operations** - delivery of operational efficiency, standardization, quality, or process excellence; durable process improvement, governance/SOP frameworks, regulated-environment readiness. File: `process-operations.md`.
- **platform-technology** - delivery of a technology platform, system, or vendor ecosystem that enables business capability; platform built or scaled, system implemented or integrated, vendor ecosystem governed. File: `platform-technology.md`.
