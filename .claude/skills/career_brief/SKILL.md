## Reserved content: Recruiter Pitch Template Customization Instructions

The instructions below are templating guidance for tailoring the Recruiter Pitch Template's variable middle to a specific role. The template body itself lives at `templates/recruiter_pitch_template.md`. Note: Step 3 references a Story-to-Theme matching table that no longer exists in positioning; the equivalent information is now the `Linked Stories:` field on each TH-NNN theme in `personal/profile/positioning.md`.

**How to Build a Variable Middle for a New Role:**

**Step 1 --- Identify the role's core problem:**

-   What are they trying to solve? What does the JD emphasize --- growth, efficiency, process maturity, technology modernization, governance? Name it in one sentence.

**Step 2 --- Match to a Signature Theme:**

-   Which of the four themes maps to their problem? If more than one, pick the most direct. That theme becomes your framing.

**Step 3 --- Select a proof point:**

-   Choose the story that most directly parallels their environment or challenge. The Story-to-Theme matching tables in this appendix can help identify it quickly. One story, done specifically, beats a general claim every time.

**Step 4 --- Watch the logical framing:**

-   If the company is growing headcount, don't say you "reduce headcount needs." Say you design processes that scale efficiently so growth is strategic, not reactive. If the company is cutting costs, lean into the efficiency angle. The same capability can be framed multiple ways --- make sure yours doesn't contradict what they're trying to do.

**Variable Middle structure example:**

*The Process Management role at AstraZeneca appeals to me specifically because the problem is one I've lived --- clinical processes that accumulate legacy assumptions faster than they get challenged. At BioMarin I redesigned data quality processes that were producing organizational comfort rather than real insight, rebuilt the governance model from scratch, and deployed it cross-portfolio. The lean methodology and KPI-based oversight language in the role description is exactly how I think about process work.*
