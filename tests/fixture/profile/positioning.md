## Positioning Statement

A clinical data leader who consolidates scattered study-level data practice into
one operated platform, and who leaves the standard documented behind them.

## Core Philosophy

- Prefer one reviewed source over several private ones.
- A standard nobody was trained on is not a standard.

## What Makes Me Unique

- Built the platform and then wrote the practice that governs it, rather than
  handing the platform over and moving on.

## Signature Themes

### TH-001 - Consolidation over standardization

**Core message:** Standardizing a scattered practice leaves the scatter in
place; consolidating removes the reconciliation step entirely.

**Proof point:** The shared warehouse build at Calder Bio (EX-001, ST-001).

**Use when:** The role names fragmented data sources, reconciliation burden, or
a first platform build.

## Elevator Statement

Ten years in clinical data, most of it spent replacing per-study improvisation
with one platform and one documented practice.
